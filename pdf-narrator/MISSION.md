# Mission

Make every technical book listenable: free, on any Android phone, without the book ever leaving it.

## Why

Technical books are one of the best ways to learn deeply, but they need a screen and a desk. Almost none of them have audiobooks, and read-aloud apps read code as a stream of symbols and skip diagrams. An engineer should be able to learn from a real book on a walk.

## Who it's for

- Developers, students and engineers who learn from technical books in PDF.
- Anyone who can't, or would rather not, read on a screen for long: commuters, people with tired eyes, dyslexic readers, and blind or low-vision developers.

## Promises

1. **The author's words, as written.** We read the prose word for word and explain only what can't be heard: code, diagrams, tables and equations. Our explanations are always marked as ours.
2. **Free, always.** No subscriptions, no paywalls, no sign-up. Reading and listening are never locked behind an ad.
3. **Private by design.** Books are processed on the phone and never uploaded.
4. **Works offline** once a book is prepared.
5. **Feels premium.** Fast, calm and polished, never cluttered or boring.
6. **Ads with respect.** Ads pay for the app, but they never play in the audio, never appear on the lock screen, and never trick anyone into a tap.

## How we decide

When goals conflict, the earlier one wins:

1. Faithful to the book: never put words in the author's mouth.
2. Listening is never interrupted.
3. Free and private.
4. Premium feel.
5. Ad revenue.

## What success looks like

- People finish chapters by ear: weekly listening hours per active user, and chapters completed.
- Explanations help: few "this explanation was wrong" flags per hour listened.
- Ads cover the app's small running costs and grow with screen-on use, without hurting how often people come back.

## What we won't do

- Upload, sell or share anyone's books or narrations.
- Distribute narrated books without the publisher's rights.
- Copy another app's brand, icons or screens, even ones we admire.
